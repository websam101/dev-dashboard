## ðŸ  Dashboard Overview
The Dashboard is your high-level summary.
- **System Stats:** View live CPU, RAM, and Disk usage.
- **Quick Shortcuts:** Instant access to your favorite projects and pinned resources.
- **Platform Info:** See your current execution mode (Electron, SSR, or SPA) and system uptime.

## ðŸ› ï¸ Project Hub
The heart of the application where you manage your local codebases.
- **Scanning:** Auto-discover projects in your managed roots.
- **Git Status:** Real-time branch tracking and commit status (ahead/behind).
- **Tech Stack:** Automatic detection of frameworks and libraries.
- **Port Radar:** Check if a port is free or already managed by another project.

## ðŸ“š Resource Manager
A central place for all your technical documentation and links.
- **Collections:** Categorize your links into folders (e.g., AI, Backend, Frontend).
- **Pinned Bar:** A context-aware bar that shows your most-used resources.
- **Metadata:** Automatic fetching of titles and descriptions for new links.

## ðŸ“Š System Monitoring
Real-time tracking of your host machine's performance.
- **Resources:** Visual indicators for CPU, RAM, and Disk.
- **Task Manager:** Quick access to your OS's native task manager.
- **Configurable:** You can hide these stats in Settings to reduce overhead.

Tailor the dashboard to your specific needs.
- **Dark Mode:** High-contrast theme for developer comfort.
- **Scan Roots:** Tell the app where your projects live.
- **Auto-check Ports:** Enable/disable background port scanning.

## ðŸ’¾ Backup & Restore
Never lose your configuration.
- **Export:** Save your entire setup (Projects, Bookmarks, Settings) to a single JSON file.
